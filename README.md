AYXchange is a mentorship app that helps students and mentors connect, learn, and create together. Finding the right person to ask for advice or share a project with can be difficult. Students may not know who has experience in a subject they care about, while mentors may want to help but need a simple way to meet learners whose goals fit their interests and availability. AYXchange brings both groups together in one welcoming online community across fields.

Members create a profile describing their interests, skills, experience, availability, and learning goals. They can choose an emoji avatar to personalize their profile. Students can browse mentor profiles and short “Mentor Moments,” where mentors share a helpful tip, a project idea, or an invitation to a learning session. 

AYXchange uses mutual matching to make connections purposeful. A student can express interest in a mentor, and a mentor can decide whether the request fits their interests and schedule. Private messaging becomes available after both people show interest. This gives members a chance to introduce themselves, ask questions, and discuss what they hope to learn before planning a session.The Warm Start feature helps conversations feel less awkward by suggesting an opening question, a simple agenda, or a small activity the pair can try together. Once matched, members can propose a meeting time and agree on a plan. Afterward, they can record what they learned, save feedback, and choose a next step in shared session notes. These notes help turn a single conversation into an ongoing learning relationship and make it easier to remember useful advice.

For example, a first-year electrical engineering student who wants to learn about electric vehicle batteries could find a mentor who works with battery testing. They might connect over a Mentor Moment about C-rate, chat about the student’s questions, and schedule a learning session. Together, they could work through a beginner-friendly calculation: a 120 amp-hour battery discharged at 60 amps has a C-rate of 0.5C. The pair could discuss what that result means, save the example, and record the mentor’s advice as a takeaway.AYXchange is designed to make mentorship approachable, organized, and useful. It brings discovery, matching, messaging, session planning, and follow-up into one experience, so a new connection can grow into a meaningful learning opportunity. Profiles show what people want to learn and what they can share. Mentor Moments make it easier to start with a concrete topic, while private chat gives matched members space to plan.
 
The current prototype uses Supabase for user accounts and saved interactions. It demonstrates the main journey from profile creation to discovery, mutual matching, messaging, session proposals, and shared notes. Its meeting room is a visual simulation; it does not provide live video or audio. Emoji avatars are available, but photo uploads are not included. Verified profiles, real video calls, calendar integrations, automated reminders, and moderation tools are possible future improvements. AYXchange aims to help people move from shared curiosity to practical collaboration, one conversation and one small project at a time.
